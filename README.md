### CLIM-000047: Definition-robust atmospheric-river change estimates
- **Subfield:** Extreme events and compound hazards
- **Open:** Establish which changes in occurrence, duration, clustering, and landfall survive reasonable detection definitions. Translate definitions into physically interpretable transport and impact statistics.
- **Status:** ARTMIP compared over twenty tracking methods in 2019 and found substantial methodological dependence.
- **Type:** data
- **Why:** Prevents event-label choices from dominating flood and water projections.
- **Key works:** Rutz et al. (2019), [“The Atmospheric River Tracking Method Intercomparison Project (ARTMIP): Quantifying Uncertainties in Atmospheric River Climatology”](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JD030936).
